## Reading the numbers

- **Saturation at c = 4 = `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL`.** Total throughput grows almost linearly
  up to 4 concurrent streams (85 → 161 → 289 tok/s) and is flat after (280–288 tok/s).
- **Beyond 4, extra concurrency only adds queueing.** TTFT jumps from 0.12 s (c=4) to 3.7 s (c=8)
  and 10.7 s (c=16): requests wait for whole generations (~3.5 s each at 256 tokens) to free a slot.
  Latency grows the same way; per-request decode speed stays at 71–86 tok/s.
- **Per-user experience is good up to 4 concurrent users:** TTFT p95 ≤ 0.13 s, ~75–86 tok/s.
- **Gateway limits match capacity:** the app keys' `max_parallel_requests` (2 + 2) sum to the 4
  slots, so under real app traffic the gateway answers excess requests with 429 immediately
  instead of letting them queue for seconds inside Ollama.

## Methodology notes (why `--restart-backend`)

The first sweep ran all levels back-to-back on one Ollama process and was **not** comparable:

| | c=1 | c=2 | c=4 | c=8 | c=16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Throughput tok/s, back-to-back sweep | 84.1 | 156.0 | 204.5 | 271.8 | 276.0 |
| TTFT p95, back-to-back sweep | 0.080 s | 0.104 s | **5.324 s** | 3.823 s | 11.457 s |

Two state-dependent effects were isolated:

1. **One-off stall at the start of a level.** In that sweep, the first 4 requests of c=4 all waited
   5.3 s together; the remaining 12 had TTFT ≈ 0.1 s. Re-running c=4 alone twice gave TTFT p95 0.12 s
   and 282 tok/s, i.e. not reproducible.
2. **c=2 degrades after oversubscription (reproducible, Ollama 0.40.18).** On a freshly restarted
   Ollama, c=2 gives 154 tok/s; after one c=8 level (more streams than slots), c=2 drops to
   ~84 tok/s total (~37 tok/s per request) and stays there until Ollama restarts. c=1 and c=4 are
   not affected. Root cause not investigated (inside Ollama/llama.cpp scheduling).

`--restart-backend` restarts Ollama and warms up before every level, so each level starts from the
same state. Treat Ollama numbers as "fresh process"; under long mixed real-world traffic,
2-stream throughput can be lower. Worth re-checking after switching to vLLM.

## Caveats

- Shared desktop machine (browser, Discord, Parsec and other GPU clients running), not a dedicated server.
- One repetition per level and small samples (8–64 requests): treat as indicative, not definitive.
- Output length fixed by `max_tokens=256` (all answers hit the cap); short answers would show
  higher request rates and lower latency.
- Context length is Ollama's default 4096; long RAG prompts (phase 2) will raise TTFT (prefill).
